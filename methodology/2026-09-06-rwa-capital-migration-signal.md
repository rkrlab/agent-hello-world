# Regime Framework Addendum — Tokenized RWA Capital Migration

Effective: 2026-09-06

Add **tokenized real-world asset capital migration** as a first-class ecosystem/regime signal.

## Signal to track by chain

Measure net growth and actual financial use of tokenized RWAs, especially:

- **Treasuries / money-market funds:** net issuance/redemptions, AUM, holder growth, secondary turnover, collateral usage, lending/borrowing integration, and settlement activity.
- **Equities / ETFs:** net issuance/redemptions, active float onchain, secondary trading volume, liquidity/depth, use as quote/collateral assets, and integration into ecosystem-native products.
- **Stablecoin settlement balances:** net supply growth by chain, bridge/import/export flows, velocity, DEX/CEX settlement usage, collateral utilization, and persistence of balances after speculative bursts.

## Quality hierarchy

Treat sustained RWA/stablecoin capital growth and utilization as a **higher-quality ecosystem signal than raw transactions, gas, wallet counts, or launch counts**, because it represents identifiable financial capital moving and being deployed onchain.

Do not equate issuance or AUM with active adoption. Separate:

1. **Issuance/AUM:** capital represented onchain.
2. **Secondary trading:** whether the assets actually trade with meaningful volume and executable liquidity.
3. **Collateral/utilization:** whether assets are used in lending, margin, AMMs, derivatives, structured products, settlement, or ecosystem-native composability.
4. **Persistence:** whether balances and utilization remain after incentives, launches, or temporary speculative events fade.

Prefer regimes where net RWA/stablecoin growth is accompanied by real secondary turnover and/or collateral use. Discount balances driven primarily by yield subsidies, points, quests, liquidity-mining programs, temporary bridge incentives, or passive issuer migration.

## Crypto-token selection remains separate

This signal improves the **WHERE / regime-quality** decision only. It must **not** bypass the existing token value-capture and structural gates.

Before surfacing a crypto token as a beneficiary, verify that ecosystem RWA growth creates a credible value-accrual path to that token (fees, burns, buybacks, required collateral, staking demand, protocol ownership, or another defensible mechanism). Do not infer token value from ecosystem growth alone.

All existing hard structural rejection gates remain mandatory and override opportunity scores.

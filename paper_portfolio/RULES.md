# Regime-Framework Paper Portfolio

Initialized: 2026-09-09T15:14:00Z
Starting NAV: $10,000.00
Base currency: USD

## Purpose

Prospectively test whether the regime/leadership framework adds value through actual paper buys and sells, without hindsight. This portfolio is separate from the user's real holdings and separate from earlier one-off paper experiments.

## Starting allocation

- USD cash: $4,000 (40%)
- BTC: $2,000 (20%)
- ETH: $2,000 (20%)
- SOL: $1,000 (10%)
- SUI: $500 (5%)
- BNB: $500 (5%)

The opening inventory is marked at the initialization prices recorded in `state.json` and the initial snapshot. No opening transaction costs are charged because these are starting holdings supplied by the user rather than trades generated by the framework.

## Trading mandate

- Spot, long-only paper trading.
- No leverage, borrowing, shorting, options, perpetuals, or other derivatives.
- A regime/leadership research alert is not automatically a buy. A paper buy requires an entry-quality decision based on the full framework at that timestamp.
- Hard structural gates are absolute. A failed structural gate prevents a buy and can force an exit from an existing position.
- Do not retroactively backdate trades to earlier signals. Execution begins only after this portfolio initialization.
- Every BUY, ADD, TRIM, or SELL is recorded before its forward outcome is known.

## Position sizing

For assets not in the opening portfolio:

- Default starter: 2.5% of current NAV.
- Confirmation add: normally another 2.5% of NAV.
- Default maximum for a non-core speculative asset: 7.5% of NAV.
- At least 10% of NAV should normally remain in cash unless an unusually strong diversified set of signals justifies otherwise.

Opening core holdings may exceed those limits because they are the user-specified starting benchmark.

## Execution and costs

Canonical mark/fill source for liquid assets: CoinGecko USD volume-weighted spot price at or immediately after the decision timestamp. If unavailable, use another independently verifiable liquid-market source and record the substitution.

Estimated one-way execution costs are charged on generated paper trades:

- Tier A: 0.15% for deep/liquid assets (normally >$1B market cap with broad venue depth).
- Tier B: 0.35% for liquid mid-cap assets.
- Tier C: at least 1.00% for small/on-chain assets, plus additional modeled price impact when executable liquidity requires it.
- If realistic executable depth is insufficient, the trade is rejected regardless of signal quality.

## Sell/trim discipline

A position can be reduced or closed when one or more of the following occurs:

- hard structural gate fails;
- regime thesis materially invalidates;
- value-capture mechanism breaks or becomes incentive-dominated;
- leadership is lost with persistent negative price/holder/liquidity confirmation;
- selective-wallet/distribution evidence materially reverses;
- crowding or liquidity deterioration makes forward asymmetry unacceptable.

A high opportunity score never overrides a structural failure.

## Evaluation

Track:

- cash, quantity, average cost, realized P&L, unrealized P&L, and NAV;
- every paper buy/sell with timestamp, price, quantity, costs, rationale, and post-trade cash;
- comparison with a passive benchmark that simply holds the exact opening allocation;
- forward outcomes without editing the original trade rationale.


## Accounting schema v2

The canonical field definitions are documented in `paper_portfolio/SCHEMA.md`.

Every hourly scan must refresh and persist position-level marks even when no trade occurs. A quiet/no-alert run may still update `state.json`; notification suppression does not mean the accounting state should remain stale.

For every open position, `state.json` must carry:

- quantity and weighted-average cost;
- total cost basis;
- current mark price, timestamp, and mark source;
- marked market value;
- unrealized P&L in USD and percent;
- realized P&L attributable to that asset;
- current portfolio weight;
- latest trade ID;
- originating/qualifying signal ID when available.

Portfolio-level state must carry the latest NAV, benchmark NAV, alpha in USD and basis points, cash, realized P&L, total unrealized P&L, and the timestamp of the full portfolio mark.

Every future BUY/ADD/TRIM/SELL ledger entry must include the associated `signal_id` (or explicitly `null` if no separate research record exists), pre/post-trade portfolio accounting, and the post-trade position accounting fields defined in the schema.

Every TRIM or SELL must explicitly record realized P&L for that transaction and cumulative realized P&L for the asset. Weighted-average cost is the paper portfolio's accounting convention unless the rules are prospectively changed and versioned.

Existing schema-v1 trade records remain immutable. Missing fields in historical records are not backfilled by rewriting the ledger; schema v2 applies prospectively.

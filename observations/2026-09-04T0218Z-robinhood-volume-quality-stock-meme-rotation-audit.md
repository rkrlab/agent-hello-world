## New contamination evidence
A commercial service, OpenLiquid, explicitly markets a Long.xyz Robinhood Chain volume bot. It allows users to choose target volume and wallet count and routes repeated on-chain swaps through Long.xyz/Uniswap pools for a fee. This means raw Long.xyz token volume, transaction counts, and even apparent wallet diversity can be manufactured without genuine independent speculative demand.

Framework change from this observation forward: Long.xyz/stock-paired token volume and unique-wallet growth receive a stronger contamination discount unless corroborated by holder persistence, independent funding, repeat accumulation, liquidity growth, and selective-wallet evidence.

## Sub-regime rotation: stock-paired memes
The stock-paired meme format itself is real and growing even though individual token metrics are contaminated. On a Sep. 1 on-chain snapshot across 19 liquid Robinhood stock tokens, 432 live pools used stock tokens as quote assets; those pools held ~17.2% of the stock-token float and generated ~31.3% of related stock-token DEX volume. At least four launchpads were using stock-paired memes as a default product.

A later report citing LONG said AI/NVDA had grown to lock ~23% of tokenized NVDA supply. The newest visible example is ICOIN/AAPL, reported on Sep. 4 at roughly $5.5M market cap, >28x 24h price change, and ~$8.9M 24h volume. This supports a genuine narrative/sub-regime rotation toward stock-paired memes, but not a qualified asset signal.

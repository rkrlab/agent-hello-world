## Why the flows appear speculative rather than incentive-led
- The UNI move is not principally tied to an UNI airdrop, points campaign, quest, or yield-farming program.
- The main catalyst is direct economic exposure to the Robinhood Chain trading boom: Uniswap v2/v3/v4 deployed on Robinhood Chain, and Uniswap governance extended protocol-fee collection / UNI-burn mechanics to the chain.
- This means continued trading activity can produce protocol fees and UNI burn regardless of which Robinhood-native meme token leads.
- Discount: the underlying Robinhood activity itself is not all high-quality discretionary speculation; prior scans found concentrated automated gas demand, correlated wallet funding, subsidies, and bot/arbitrage activity. The UNI thesis therefore depends partly on the persistence of economically meaningful Robinhood volume, not merely headline transaction counts.

## Leadership / rotation evidence
- PONS remains the native Robinhood flagship and has not been displaced.
- UNI is emerging as a separate infrastructure leadership layer: capital can rotate from picking an individual meme winner toward the venue/token that captures fee value across the ecosystem.
- UNI has persisted above roughly $6 after a multi-week re-rating from roughly $4.27 on Aug 25, with current market capitalization around $3.9B and roughly $0.9B-$1.0B daily token turnover.
- This is a spillover/secondary-leadership signal, not evidence that UNI has replaced PONS as Robinhood's speculative flagship.

## Structural gate
Result: PASS WITH GOVERNANCE-MINT CAVEAT.
- Canonical long-lived verified ERC-20; no microcap-style deployer LP-removal or honeypot risk identified.
- Original team / investor / advisor vesting concluded in September 2025.
- There is no active scheduled inflation under current policy.
- Governance retains a mint function capped at 2% of total supply per 365-day period; official Uniswap documentation states that governance has never exercised it. This is a known governance dilution risk, not a hidden mint authority.
- No freeze authority / creator-controlled liquidity issue comparable to the rejected launch-token candidates was identified.

## Major uncertainties
- Robinhood's headline activity quality remains partially contaminated by bots, arbitrage, subsidies, and correlated wallets.
- Selective-wallet independence and historical skill are not yet proven.
- UNI price has already materially re-rated, reducing prospective asymmetry versus the start of the move.
- A large-wallet deposit to Binance is a genuine counter-signal.
- Fee accrual / burn is economically cleaner than meme-token turnover, but burn magnitude may be too small relative to UNI's market capitalization if Robinhood activity fades.

## Why the signal matters now
Earlier runs had an active UNI infrastructure-spillover thesis but insufficient wallet confirmation. The combination now includes persistent price/volume re-rating, direct Robinhood protocol-fee/burn linkage, expanding whale/fresh-wallet accumulation, strong executability, and a structural gate that passes. This is enough to cross the research alert threshold even though selective-wallet convergence remains only moderate and PONS remains the native-token leader.

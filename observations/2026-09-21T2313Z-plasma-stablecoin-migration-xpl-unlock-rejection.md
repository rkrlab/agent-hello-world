## Prospective observation

Plasma is the cleanest new retained-capital mover in this scan, but the migration currently looks primarily like stablecoin/payment/settlement liquidity rather than discretionary speculative capital. DefiLlama showed approximately $1.256B stablecoins (+15.78% over 7d), $557.68M DeFi TVL, $10.39M 24h DEX volume, $59.77M 7d DEX volume (+56.30%), 33,596 active addresses, 8,892 new addresses, and 579,412 transactions. RWA Active AUM was ~$44.56M and is treated as issuance/AUM, not secondary trading or collateral usage.

The quality distinction is important: $10.39M of daily DEX volume is only about 0.83% of the $1.256B stablecoin stock, while chain fees were only about $361/day. The stablecoin stock therefore shows real retained capital but not, by itself, a high-velocity speculative regime. Circle's Aug. 28 launch of native USDC/EURC plus CCTP provides a financially substantive payments/settlement catalyst independent of memecoin churn.

XPL does **not** qualify for a paper buy. CoinGecko showed XPL around $0.097-$0.100 with ~$122M of 24h token turnover, ~2.8B circulating supply, ~$270M market cap and ~$973M FDV. More importantly, its Sep. 25 unlock releases ~1.76B XPL: ~833.33M to investors, ~833.33M to team, and ~88.89M ecosystem/growth. That is roughly 63% of the current ~2.8B circulating base. Plasma's official tokenomics allocate 25% of initial supply to team and 25% to investors, each with one-third unlocking after a one-year cliff and the rest monthly over two years. Under the framework's hard supply/holder-concentration gate, this immediate insider/investor overhang blocks a speculative entry regardless of ecosystem growth.

Value capture is also weaker than chain activity suggests. XPL secures Plasma and supports validator incentives/governance, but Plasma deliberately sponsors zero-fee USDT transfers and supports custom gas tokens/paymasters, reducing the requirement that stablecoin users acquire XPL for every transaction. Plasma One can create token-lock demand (e.g. Core via a 12-month XPL lock), but that is product-incentive/utility demand and must be separated from broad discretionary speculation.

No independent cohort of historically selective, independently funded XPL spot accumulators was verified in this run. Raw price/volume strength therefore does not override the structural gate.

## Cross-chain context

Broad market retained capital remains weak relative to leverage: aggregate stablecoin supply was ~flat (+0.01% 7d), DEX volume was only +1.00% weekly, while perp volume was +20.91%. Hyperliquid remains the strongest established continuation regime (+5.76% stablecoins, +34.28% spot DEX, +36.73% perps over 7d), but no fresh independent selective-wallet accumulation was verified to justify another HYPE add. Base, Solana, Arbitrum, BSC, Avalanche, Sui, Polygon and other scanned ecosystems did not produce a cleaner new prospective trade after incentive/flow-quality filtering.

## Forward test

Reconsider Plasma/XPL only after the Sep. 25 unlock is absorbed without structural distribution damage and if: (1) stablecoin balances remain elevated after launch/product incentives, (2) DEX and lending/collateral velocity rise materially relative to stablecoin stock, (3) RWA secondary trading or collateral utilization becomes measurable rather than just AUM, (4) independent selective-wallet accumulation appears, and (5) XPL holder-level value capture strengthens enough to connect payment growth to token demand.
